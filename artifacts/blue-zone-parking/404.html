Set your disc
Set to
—

Set the wheel to the next half-hour mark after you arrive.
Example: arrive 9:05 → set 9:30 → leave by 10:30.

Rules

Time limits (Mon–Sat)

  • 08:00–11:30 & 13:30–18:00 → max 1 hour
  • Arrival 11:30–13:30 → valid until 14:30
  • Arrival 18:00–08:00 → valid until 09:00

Sundays & public holidays

No time limit unless an extra sign says otherwise.

Setting the disc

Set the wheel to the next time-line after arrival. Electronic discs are not permitted. Disc must be clearly visible and upright.

No disc needed

19:00–07:59 if you rejoin traffic before 08:00.

Invalid if

  • Slanted or not fully visible
  • Arrival time set incorrectly
  • Max parking time exceeded
  • Time re-adjusted without rejoining traffic

Where to get a disc

  • Transport Service licensing office — Mühlegasse 18
  • Zurich City Police guards
  • Specialist retailers

Legal basis: Signaling Ordinance of 5 September 1979. Always follow local signs.
